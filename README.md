CarbonIQ connects everyday electricity use to the carbon behind it. It pulls real-world data from the Ember Energy API and offers three tools:

Footprint Calculator: enter your monthly kWh and pick your country. It multiplies your usage by the country's latest grid carbon intensity (gCO₂/kWh) to estimate your footprint.
Explore Your Energy: interactive charts of a country's electricity generation by source (coal, gas, hydro, solar, wind, nuclear and more), monthly or yearly from 1990. You can switch to a fossil vs. renewables vs. nuclear ratio view.
Trace Your Country's Carbon: a timeline of power-sector CO₂ emissions broken down by coal, gas, other fossil and clean sources.
It's built with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Bootstrap 5 and Chart.js.
